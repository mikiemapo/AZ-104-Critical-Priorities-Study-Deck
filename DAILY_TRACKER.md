## 📞 Quick Help: What to Do If...

**"I'm too tired"**

- Take the free day (Wed or Sat)
- Or: 30-min light Anki instead of 5-hour session
- Never push through exhaustion; recovery > grind

**"I don't understand a concept"**

- Watch Deep Dive audio (if available)
- Check Media Hub visual
- Re-read explanation in Anki card
- Google [Azure concept] + "exam tips"

**"My score isn't improving"**

- Review wrong answers; don't retake same exam immediately
- Spend 1 day on one topic only
- Use Media Hub for visual understanding
- Take it to the Deep Dive audio if available

**"I'm ahead of schedule"**

- Generate Anki decks from additional Deep Dive transcripts
- Complete extra Whizlabs labs
- Do practice exams early to identify weak areas sooner

**"I'm behind schedule"**

- Cut optional Deep Dives (keep core: VMSS, App Service, Storage)
- Focus on highest-point exam domains (Storage, Compute, Networking)
- Extend into Week 9 if needed
- Don't rush; better to delay exam 1 week than fail it
